/ THE OVERVIEW
Cendrol Construct is a construction and interior solutions company managing distributed on-site operations.
My contribution focused on redesigning the expense reimbursement experience to simplify tracking, approvals, and reimbursement workflows for on-site engineers.
/ THE CHALLENGE
Engineers found it difficult to track submitted expenses and understand reimbursement status across multiple project sites.
The existing workflow lacked visibility, making approvals and reimbursements feel disconnected and difficult to follow.
/ THE SOLUTION PATH
Chapter 1: Simplifying expense tracking
Making submissions easier to manage
Expense submissions were spread across multiple stages, making tracking difficult during active site operations.
The experience was redesigned to simplify submission flows and reduce operational friction during reimbursement requests.
Designing for admin-side tracking and approvals
Managing reimbursements across multiple projects required clearer visibility into request status, approval stages, and operational activity.
The admin experience introduced structured tracking, request filtering, and approval visibility to help teams monitor reimbursement workflows more efficiently.
Improving reimbursement visibility
Engineers often struggled to understand approval status after submitting expenses. The system introduced clearer reimbursement states and approval visibility across the workflow.
Chapter 2: Creating clearer reimbursement workflows
Reducing confusion across approval stages
The earlier workflow lacked clear progression between submission, approval, and reimbursement stages.
We introduced structured workflow states that helped engineers understand where requests stood within the process.
Making reimbursement progress easier to follow
Managing reimbursements across distributed project sites often felt fragmented. The redesigned experience surfaced approval progress, reimbursement status, and pending actions more clearly.
Chapter 3: Designing for faster operational clarity
Reducing interpretation effort
The interface focused on simplifying information hierarchy across expense tracking and approval flows.
Clearer layouts, status visibility, and structured interaction patterns improved scanability during day-to-day operations.
Improving usability across distributed operations
Engineers frequently managed reimbursements while coordinating across active project sites.
The experience was designed to support quicker tracking, easier navigation, and reduced workflow friction across operational environments.
/ CONCLUSION
The project transformed reimbursement tracking from a fragmented operational workflow into a clearer and more structured experience.
By improving submission visibility, approval tracking, and reimbursement clarity, the system helped engineers manage operational expenses more confidently across distributed project sites.
